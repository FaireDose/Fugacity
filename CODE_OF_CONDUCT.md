# Code of conduct

CHEPTA is built by students, engineers, academics and hobbyists from many countries.
To keep it a place where everyone can contribute:

- **Be respectful.** Criticise data and code, not people. Assume good intent.
- **Be precise and honest.** Cite sources, state uncertainty, and say when you are not sure.
  Never present numbers you have not checked.
- **Give credit.** Name the authors of the data and ideas you build on.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Explain, point to documentation, and be patient with first
  contributions.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing
someone's private information, and deliberately submitting false data.

Report problems privately to a maintainer (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)). Maintainers
may remove comments, close pull requests, or block people who break these rules, and
will explain their decision to those involved.
