# Excel concept model: recalculated in LibreOffice

Generated by `validation/excel/check_concept.py`. Each workbook is the "Excel concept model" export of the flowsheet (`src/ui/flowsheet-excel.js`), recalculated by LibreOffice with every formula evaluated. "As exported" must reproduce Fugacity's solution (it checks the formulas). After an edit, the workbook is compared with Fugacity run again with the same change: that difference is the concept-design approximation (activity coefficients and, for a component without a vapour pressure at the drum temperature, K held at the exported state). Deviation: the largest difference of a component flow, relative to that stream's total flow.
